SECTION 5: TROUBLESHOOTING
=================================================================

Issue:
Device Offline

Possible Causes:

- Router rebooted
- Password changed
- Weak signal

Resolution:

1. Verify internet connection.
2. Restart router.
3. Reconnect device.
4. Update firmware.

------------------------------------------------------------

Issue:
Bluetooth Pairing Failed

Resolution:

1. Enable Bluetooth.
2. Remove existing pairings.
3. Move closer to device.
4. Retry setup.

------------------------------------------------------------

Issue:
Firmware Update Failure

Resolution:

1. Verify storage availability.
2. Reboot device.
3. Retry update.
4. Contact support.
